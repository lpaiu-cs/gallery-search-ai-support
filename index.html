AI photo search for iPhone

Find the photo you have in mind.

Describe a scene in English. On-device AI searches the photos you choose and ranks the closest matches. Your photo collection and search stay on your iPhone.

How it worksPrivacy details

Search by what you remember

Try a description such as “a dog at the beach” or “a red car in snow.” Search runs against the photos you selected for your collection.

Private, on-device AI

The app includes its search model. Your photos, search descriptions, and search representations are not uploaded to cloud AI or a developer server. You decide which photos to add and when to share a match with another app.

Prepare once. Find again.

On-device AI prepares selected photos for search. The app keeps completed work on your iPhone, so reopening the app does not require indexing those photos again. Search results can be shared or copied when you need them.
