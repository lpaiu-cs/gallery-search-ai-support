Gallery Search AI — Privacy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Photos and search

Gallery Search AI processes your chosen photos and English search descriptions entirely on your iPhone. Its AI search models are included in the app. The app does not upload your photos, descriptions, or search representations to cloud AI, the developer, or a search service. It has no advertising, analytics service, app account, or tracking identifier.

Photo access

You choose which photos the app may access, individually or by a date range. Date selection reads photo dates from the library you authorize. Limited photo-library access is supported; full-library access is not required. Indexing asks Photos for previews already available locally and does not request iCloud originals. Photos and iOS may separately synchronize your library under your system settings. A photo with no available local preview remains waiting.

What stays on your device

The app saves small image copies, search vectors, photo-library identifiers, preview metadata, content fingerprints used to avoid duplicate imports, and a list of photos still waiting for access or a preview. These records stay in the app's private storage and are excluded from device backups. Saved preview copies omit embedded source metadata. Search descriptions are used for the current search and are not stored as a search history.

Trial and purchases

Apple handles the free trial and one-time unlock through the App Store. The app reads verified StoreKit transactions on your device to determine trial eligibility and purchase access. It also stores an app-only time record on your device to help enforce the trial duration. The developer does not receive your payment details or send these transaction records to a developer-operated server. Starting a trial, purchasing, or restoring purchases may communicate with Apple and uses your Apple Account under Apple's privacy practices. There is no subscription or automatic charge after the trial.

Sharing and copying

When you choose Share or Copy, the app may request the selected full-size photo from Photos (including current edits), including an iCloud download if necessary. Sharing hands that photo to the destination you choose in the iOS share sheet. Copying writes it to the system clipboard, where another app may read it when you paste. Recipients and other apps apply their own privacy practices. The shared or copied photo may contain metadata such as capture time or location. The developer does not receive a copy.

Your choices and deletion

Change photo permission in iOS Settings. Removing an app copy or clearing the app's library deletes the corresponding private search data without deleting your originals in Photos. Removing the app removes its private storage; it does not erase purchase history held by Apple or start a new trial. Previously shared photos and clipboard content are outside the app's private library; manage those in the receiving app or system. Data removal, help, and privacy information remain available after a trial ends.
